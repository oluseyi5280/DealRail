Customer discovery

The deal they lost is the only deal worth asking about.

Won deals tell you what people tolerate. Lost deals tell you where the work actually broke — who stalled, at what moment, what they tried instead of you, and what they would have paid to never feel that again. Log one interview here while it's still fresh.

  • ~4 minutes per record
  • 12 fields, all in their words
  • No recording, no transcript

What every record needs

  1. 1The agency — size and who actually owns the business.
  2. 2The last deal they lost — the specific one, not an average.
  3. 3Where it stalled — the exact moment it went sideways.
  4. 4What they did next — the workaround you're quietly competing with.
  5. 5Their words — verbatim, unedited, no paraphrase.
  6. 6Willingness to pay — a number and how confidently they said it.

Before you hit save

  • It's a specific deal with a rough number attached, not "we lose deals on price".
  • The stall point is a moment in time, not a general feeling.
  • The quote is copied, not summarised. Awkward phrasing is the useful part.
  • There's a price and an honest note about how firmly they said it.
